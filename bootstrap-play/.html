Bootstrap splits every row into 12 invisible columns. By combining class numbers (like col-4 and col-8), you control exact screen ratios.

The md part means "Medium screens and wider" (laptops/desktops). On smaller mobile screens, the column automatically expands to 100% full width so content stacks cleanly!
